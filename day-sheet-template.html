What belongs on a day sheet

A day sheet is a schedule, not a novel. Keep it to what someone actually needs to know without scrolling:

  • Venue and address — with a tappable link to directions, not a copy-pasted string.
  • The schedule — load-in, soundcheck, doors, set, and load-out. Five times, nothing more.
  • Production contact — one name and number for whoever's running the room that night.
  • Lodging — where the band's actually sleeping, if it's not straight to the next town.

Everything else — the stage plot, the rider, the guest list — belongs one tap away, not crammed onto the same page.
