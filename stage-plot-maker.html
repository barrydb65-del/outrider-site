What actually needs to be on a stage plot

A stage plot only has to answer one question fast: where does everything go, and what does it need? At minimum, that means:

  • Every input — each amp, drum mic, vocal mic, and DI, positioned where it actually sits on stage.
  • Monitor positions — how many wedges, and who's getting what mix, or where the in-ear packs go.
  • Power and cable runs — anything the house crew needs to know before they start pulling cable.
  • Upstage/audience orientation — so nobody sets up your stage plot mirrored.

Pair it with an input list and hospitality notes — Outrider calls that combination a tech/hospitality rider — and the house engineer has everything they need before you've even loaded in.
