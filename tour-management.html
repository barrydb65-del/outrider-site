One place for every show

Most bands aren't running their shows out of one system - they're running them out of five. A text thread for load-in time. An email for the rider. A spreadsheet for the run. Screenshots of a PDF nobody can find when they need it. Outrider replaces the scattered version with one place: every show, its schedule, and everyone who needs to see it.

What's a "tour" in Outrider?

A tour doesn't have to mean a tour bus, 50 cities, and a tour manager. It can be a weekend run. A month of club dates. Six shows around the Northeast. Or sixty dates across the country. If you're playing a series of shows, Outrider gives you one place to organize all the details - wherever the road takes you.

In Outrider, a single date is a show. A string of dates is a run. However big it gets from there, it's a tour. Set up whichever one you're actually playing - the tools work the same either way.
